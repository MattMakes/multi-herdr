## Goal

The validator refuses a plan in which a requirement has no task that covers it, a task covers an unknown requirement, or a requirement cites no approved assumption or answer.

## Requirements

- FR-3 (the coverage check)
- FM-1
- FM-30 (citations to approved assumptions)
- I3 at plan time
