# Read the numbers as a designer (method step 11)

This file belongs to the `godot-upgrade-tree-design` skill. The terms (diag tree, chapter, class, policy) are defined in its SKILL.md.

- Pace: a SLOW chapter with a WALL mark needs a fight answer; a SLOW chapter without one needs cheaper early levels or more income.
- Spoke worth: the top leave-one-out spokes go in the first ring; a spoke with lost progress is required and must be obvious; a spoke worth about 0 is a cut or merge candidate.
- Order per class: use it for the ring order.
- Freedom: many near-optimal policies mean real choice; 1 dominant policy is a dominant strategy to guide or break.
- Spikes: put a big power jump where a wall breaks.
- Rare currencies: put rare-currency gates where the bank can pay them.
- Check: each reading is a `notes` line.
