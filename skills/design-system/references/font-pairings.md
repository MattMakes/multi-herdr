# Font pairings

Heading and body pairings by product and mood, curated from a 74-pairing dataset. All families in the tables are on Google Fonts. Pick one pairing, then load at most 2 families and 4 weight files.

## Choose

1. Match the product first, the mood second. A dashboard needs a legible sans at small sizes before it needs character.
2. One family with several weights is a valid system (Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, IBM Plex Sans, Geist).
3. Contrast the pair on one axis only: serif against sans, or geometric against humanist. Two display faces never pair.
4. Display faces (Bebas Neue, Abril Fatface, Anton, Press Start 2P, script faces) are for short headlines only, at 24 px and up. Never for body, labels, or buttons.
5. Check the glyph set: the languages you ship, tabular figures for data, and the weights you need.
6. Serif body text is for long reading (articles, documentation prose). Product UI, forms, and dashboards use a sans body.

## Distinctive alternatives

Common defaults (Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, Poppins) read as generic on brand and marketing surfaces. They remain good choices for dense product UI. When the brief asks for character, consider:

| Role | Families | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral sans with character | Geist, Manrope, Plus Jakarta Sans, Figtree, Instrument Sans | Google Fonts |
| Geometric display sans | Outfit, Space Grotesk, Syne, Bricolage Grotesque | Google Fonts |
| Modern display serif | Fraunces, Instrument Serif, Newsreader, Young Serif | Google Fonts |
| Mono for data and code | Geist Mono, JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono, DM Mono | Google Fonts |
| Premium sans (license check) | Satoshi, Cabinet Grotesk, General Sans | Fontshare (free license, not Google) |

Check the license of every family before you ship it. Self-host when the product has privacy or offline needs.

## Type rules that apply to every pairing

- Body 16 px minimum (16 px on mobile inputs stops iOS zoom), line height 1.5 to 1.75, measure 60 to 75 characters (`max-width: 65ch`).
- Headings 600 to 700 with line height 1.1 to 1.3; display tracking about -0.02em. Body tracking 0. All caps +0.05em and short.
- Weights: do not use weights under 400 below 16 px. Use 500 for labels and buttons.
- Numbers in tables, prices, and timers: `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.
- Short headings: `text-wrap: balance`. Prose: `text-wrap: pretty` where supported. Long URLs and IDs: `overflow-wrap: anywhere`.
- Load with `font-display: swap`, preload only the critical file, and set a metric-matched fallback (`size-adjust`, or `next/font` which does it for you).

## Loading example

```html
<link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.googleapis.com">
<link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.gstatic.com" crossorigin>
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Space+Grotesk:wght@500;700&family=DM+Sans:wght@400;500&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```

```css
:root {
  --font-display: "Space Grotesk", ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif;
  --font-body: "DM Sans", ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif;
  --font-mono: "JetBrains Mono", ui-monospace, monospace;
}
```

## Checks

- Render the type scale on one page (display to caption) in both fonts at 375 px and 1440 px.
- Count loaded font files in the network panel: 4 or fewer for most sites.
- Measure layout shift when fonts swap; keep CLS under 0.1.
