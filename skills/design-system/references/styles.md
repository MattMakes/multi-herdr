# Style catalog

Visual styles with where they fit, where they fail, the token decisions they imply, and their accessibility risk. Curated from an 88-style dataset (deprecated rows and landing-page section patterns removed). Use it at step 2 to name a direction and to see what it does to tokens. For art direction and taste judgment, use the `art-direction` or `ui-taste` skill if available.

## How to use

1. Shortlist 2 or 3 styles whose "Fits" column matches the product and whose "Avoid for" column does not.
2. Translate the token cues into real scale values: radius, shadow or elevation, border width, motion durations, density. The cues are intent, not literal CSS.
3. Combine at most one primary style with one secondary trait (for example "Minimalism with Bento Box Grid layout"). Do not mix flat and skeuomorphic surfaces at the same level.
4. Risk `high` or `conditional` means the style needs extra work to pass: contrast on translucent or textured surfaces, a reduced-motion path, a reduced-transparency fallback, and visible focus on low-contrast fills. List that work in `DESIGN.md`.

Risk legend: `low` passes with normal care; `conditional` passes only with the listed mitigations; `high` fails by default for text-heavy or accessibility-critical products.

## Platform design systems

When the product lives inside a platform, follow that system instead of a style from the catalog: Material 3 (Android, Wear OS), Apple Human Interface Guidelines and Liquid Glass (Apple platforms), Fluent 2 (Microsoft 365, Windows, Copilot surfaces), Shopify Polaris (Shopify admin apps, checkout, POS), Adobe Spectrum or Spectrum 2 (Adobe-adjacent creative tools). Map their tokens into the semantic layer; do not restyle their components.

## Mobile-specific variants

The dataset also holds mobile adaptations of several styles: Bauhaus, Minimalist Monochrome, Modern Dark (cinema), SaaS boutique, Terminal CLI, Kinetic Brutalism, Flat touch-first, Material 3 Expressive, Neo Brutalism, Bold Typography poster, Academia, Cyberpunk HUD, Bitcoin DeFi, Claymorphism, Enterprise SaaS, Sketch hand-drawn, Neumorphism. They follow the same rules as their web parents, plus: 44 pt (iOS) or 48 dp (Android) targets, safe areas, platform gestures, and Dynamic Type or font scaling.

## Style mitigations

| Style trait | Required mitigation | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Translucent or glass surfaces | Solid fallback under `prefers-reduced-transparency`; text on an opaque or strongly tinted layer | Contrast measured over the busiest real background |
| Soft or low-contrast shadows (neumorphism, soft UI) | A visible border or 3:1 boundary on controls; strong focus ring | Controls distinguishable in grayscale |
| Neon, glow, glitch, scanlines | Reserve for decoration; body text plain; reduced-motion disables flicker | No flashing above 3 times per second |
| Heavy motion (parallax, kinetic type, perpetual loops) | Reduced-motion path with the final state shown; pause controls on auto-play | Page usable with reduced motion on |
| Texture and grain overlays | `pointer-events: none`, fixed layer, low opacity | Text contrast measured with the overlay on |
| Custom cursors | Keep the system cursor available; never hide it on text inputs | Keyboard and touch flows unaffected |
| Oversized display type | `clamp()` bounds; headings wrap cleanly at 375 px | No horizontal scroll at 375 px |
