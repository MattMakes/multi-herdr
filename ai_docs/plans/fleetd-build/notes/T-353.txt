NOTE from the orchestrator for T-353 (from the review of T-291, findings F1 and F4):
Today horch ledger handoff releases the tree and the lease, and wakes the next claim, before the pane of the old attempt is closed. The old process can still write the tree for a short time. T-291 kept this window because a strict refusal breaks runs with no fleetd.
In your task, "stops before launches" must close this window for attempts that fleetd launches: fleetd must stop the old attempt by its recorded PID, and see that it is gone, before it launches the next attempt on the same tree. Add 1 test: a handoff with a live old process gives no launch of the next attempt until the old process is gone.

The task that launches an attempt must set HORCH_TASK_YAML in the pane or process environment of the attempt. Check it and add 1 test.

Also: the adopted quota restart keeps the old claim path until your ticket replaces it with bind and --attempt; remove the private claim line constant of the adopt path then.
