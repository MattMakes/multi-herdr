

Fleet skill phase: implementation. Skills you are expected to use on this task. Load each one's body when its step comes up, not all at startup:
- horch:create-plan: Use when a design or requirements are settled and need executable implementation tasks with verification.
- horch:pre-flight: Use before executing a plan to verify wiring, contracts, preservation, configuration, and prerequisites against source.
